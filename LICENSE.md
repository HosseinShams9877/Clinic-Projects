## 8. Termination

Rights granted under an agreement terminate automatically on breach, and on
non-renewal. On termination:

- use of the software must cease;
- the hosted service's access is revoked;
- an on-premise installation's licence key ceases to validate on its expiry;
- the clinic's data is made available for export and then deleted per the
  agreement's retention terms.

**Termination does not destroy the clinic's data as leverage.** The product
degrades on an expired licence rather than withholding records
([`docs/setup/licensing.md`](docs/setup/licensing.md) §4).
